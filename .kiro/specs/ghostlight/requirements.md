# Ghostlight requirements

Planning input authored by Codex, November 24, 2026. Not a record of Kiro execution.

1. A visitor can choose three authored outage scenarios without signing in.
2. Every scenario exposes symptoms, trace inspection, interventions, feedback, and prevention guidance.
3. Correct containment sets health to 100 and locks further actions; incorrect actions reduce score with a floor of zero.
4. Evidence gathering before containment avoids the guessing penalty.
5. Refresh restores a validated action sequence. Corrupt or unavailable storage must not prevent use.
6. Export produces a Markdown timeline and current outcome.
7. UI supports mobile layouts, keyboard focus, reduced motion and live feedback.
8. The app never accesses production systems or claims actual monitoring.
